Research

Satellite imagery · GIS

Generalizable slum segmentation via remote sensing

Informal settlements lack reliable geospatial datasets, which makes global poverty mapping difficult. I build datasets and models that generalize across regions without local labels, using a Mixture-of-Experts design to capture regional visual variation and Test-Time Adaptation for zero-shot transfer to unseen geographies.

LLM agents · ongoing

LLM corruption through bribery game theory

Language models are increasingly placed in public-facing roles as neutral arbiters, yet they can act self-interestedly under the right conditions. I use a bribery game framework, where rational agents have no incentive to deviate, to isolate when and why models behave corruptly. The setup extends naturally to LLM–human interaction in governance-like settings.
